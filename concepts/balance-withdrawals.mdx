Your yuan balance grows automatically each time a PIX charge you created is confirmed as paid. When you're ready to convert that balance back to BRL, call `POST /v1/withdrawals` with the amount and a destination PIX key — noob-exchange handles the conversion and pushes the funds to the recipient.

## How Balance Accumulates

Every confirmed payment adds its `yuan_value` to your account balance. To check your current balance at any time:

```
GET /v1/balance
```

Response:

```json
{
  "yuan_balance": 342.50
}
```

Only charges with status `paid` contribute to your balance. `expired` and `cancelled` charges have no effect.

## Requesting a Withdrawal

Specify the amount you want to withdraw (`yuan_value`) and the destination PIX key (`pix_key`). The yuan is **reserved immediately** and the BRL equivalent is calculated using the quote active at the moment of the request — that quote is then frozen for the withdrawal.

**Request body:**

```json
{
  "yuan_value": 50,
  "pix_key": "user@example.com"
}
```

**Response:**

```json
{
  "id": "wd_01J3K8MPQR4XYZABCDEF",
  "yuan_value": 50,
  "value_real": 41.67,
  "quote": 1.2,
  "pix_key": "user@example.com",
  "status": "pending"
}
```

Use `GET /v1/withdrawals` to list your 50 most recent withdrawal requests and track their progress.

## Withdrawal Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `pending` | Withdrawal received; awaiting processing by the administrator. |
| `paid` | BRL has been sent to the specified PIX key. |
| `rejected` | Withdrawal was rejected by the administrator; the reserved yuan has been returned to your balance. |

## Withdrawal Limits

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum per request | 1 yuan |
| Maximum per request | 100,000 yuan |
| Rate limit | 10 withdrawals per hour per API key |

<Warning>
  Your yuan balance is reserved the moment a withdrawal request is submitted — it will not be available for new withdrawals while the request is `pending`. If the administrator rejects the withdrawal, the reserved yuan is automatically returned to your balance in full.
</Warning>
