noob-exchange is a currency exchange API built for developers. Accept payments in Brazilian reais (BRL) via PIX, and the equivalent value in yuan is credited to your balance automatically — no manual conversion, no waiting. When you're ready, withdraw your yuan balance to any PIX key in seconds.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Payment">
    Call `POST /v1/payments` with the amount in BRL. The API returns a PIX QR code and copy-paste code ready to share with your payer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payer Completes the PIX Transfer">
    The payer scans the QR code and sends the BRL amount from any PIX-enabled bank or wallet. Payments expire after 10 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Yuan Is Credited to Your Balance">
    Once the PIX transfer is confirmed, noob-exchange converts the BRL at the current rate and credits the equivalent yuan to your account balance instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw to Any PIX Key">
    Call `POST /v1/withdrawals` with a destination PIX key (CPF, phone, e-mail, or random key) to transfer your yuan balance out at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
